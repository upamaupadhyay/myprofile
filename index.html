Who I Am

Turning Data into Decisions

From raw market data to executive-level insights — I own the entire journey.

I am a Principal Data Analyst at NielsenIQ (GfK), where I have spent 7+ years building expertise across the complete NielsenIQ analytics ecosystem. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous data engineering and commercial strategy — transforming raw retail and consumer data into intelligence that shapes business outcomes for global FMCG and retail brands.

My day-to-day centres on consulting directly with clients to capture their exact analytical requirements, then orchestrating a team of 9 analysts through every stage of the data pipeline — ingestion, extrapolation modelling, database mapping, comprehensive quality assurance, and final delivery via Newron and Power BI dashboards.

Three company awards — including the Client Obsessed Award (2026) — reflect my commitment to delivery quality and client partnership. I am known for making quality and accountability the team default, not the exception.
